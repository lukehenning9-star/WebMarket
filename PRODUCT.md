# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Independent restaurant owners — not tech-savvy, often viewing on a phone after service. Slightly anxious about their digital presence. They want to find their restaurant's website already built, confirm it looks professional, and understand how to get it live. They need reassurance and competence, not enthusiasm.

## Product Purpose

A marketplace landing page where restaurant owners search their name, find a professionally-built website already made for them, preview it in a full iframe, and contact to purchase. One flat fee, no subscriptions. Core value: the work is already done before the owner arrives. The search bar is the product.

## Brand Personality

Capable, direct, warm. Like handing someone a finished product and saying "here it is." Not hustle, not hype — quiet confidence that earns trust. Three words: professional, considered, direct.

## Anti-references

- Orange/charcoal — too aggressive, reads like a sale flyer
- Forest green on cream — trendy without purpose, wrong register
- Void-black with glassmorphism nav — SaaS/tech register mismatch for this audience
- Editorial-typographic monochrome (Klim/Newsreader style) — saturated AI tell for 2026, wrong audience
- SaaS hero-metric template — big numbers in identical cards, generic everywhere

## Design Principles

1. The search bar is the product. Every other element defers to it visually.
2. Trust through competence. Show quality, not enthusiasm. No buzzwords.
3. Warmth from the brand color, not the surface. Pure white backgrounds; oxblood does the identity work.
4. Direct. Every word on the page earns its place. No filler, no restated headings.
5. Works for tired restaurant owners on phones after a long service shift.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Body text contrast ≥ 4.5:1. Touch targets ≥ 44px. Keyboard navigable. Reduced motion respected via prefers-reduced-motion.
