What we offer

Professional grooming, done right.

Full Groom

Our most complete service. Bath, blow-dry, breed-standard or custom haircut, ear cleaning, nail trim, and finishing touches. Your dog leaves looking — and smelling — their best.

Bath & Brush

A thorough bath with professional shampoo and conditioner, full blow-dry, and brush-out. Great for maintenance between full grooms, or for short-coated breeds that just need a solid refresh.

Nail Trim

Safe, quick nail trimming by experienced hands. We work calmly with anxious dogs. Add a nail grind for a smooth finish that won't scratch floors or skin.

Ear Cleaning

Gentle, thorough ear cleaning to remove buildup and reduce the risk of irritation or infection. Especially important for floppy-eared breeds and dogs with a history of ear issues.

Pricing is based on breed, coat condition, and size. Call (316) 390-6751 and we'll give you a straightforward quote.

About us

A studio built on trust, not volume.

Keep it Cute Grooming has been part of the Wichita community for nearly a decade. What started as a genuine love for animals and a commitment to doing the work well has grown into a studio where dogs are cared for — not just processed.

We're certified in over 130 breeds, which means we understand the specific demands of your dog's coat type, temperament, and individual history. Whether you have a first-time puppy who's nervous about the dryer, a senior dog who needs extra patience, or a high-maintenance double coat that other groomers avoid — we've handled it before, and we do it well.

Our pricing is honest. We're not the cheapest in Wichita, and we're not trying to be. But we're accessible, we're consistent, and we stand behind every appointment. Ninety-two percent of our clients recommend us. We're proud of that number.
