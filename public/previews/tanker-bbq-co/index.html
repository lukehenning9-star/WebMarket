Our Story

Passion on
Every Plate

Tanker BBQ Co. is where Wichita comes for real barbecue. Located on West Maple, every dish starts with premium ingredients and ends with that unmistakable smoke ring you can only get from cooking it right.

The owner's dedication to the craft shows in every bite — from brisket that melts on your tongue to handmade sides that keep customers coming back week after week. Friendly faces, a clean space, and unforgettable food.
