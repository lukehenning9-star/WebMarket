The kitchen

Real Southern cooking,
served with soul.

The Eatery has been feeding Wichita with honest, made-from-scratch soul food— the kind that takes time and shows it. Whether it’s the golden catfish that regulars drive across town for, the wings that earn perfect tens, or the homemade okra and cornbread that taste like somebody’s grandmother’s kitchen, this is food that means something.

Located on E. 13th Street, The Eatery serves generous portions at honest prices. Delivery and takeout available. The staff are warm, the food is never rushed, and every plate leaves the kitchen with care.
