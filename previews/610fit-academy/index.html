Meet Jenny & the 610Fit Academy

610Fit Academy was born from a simple but powerful belief: your body is a temple, and caring for it is an act of worship.

Located on Austin Avenue in the heart of Waco, Texas, 610Fit is more than a training studio — it's a community built around purpose. Jenny brings years of personal training experience alongside a deep faith conviction that how we treat our bodies directly reflects how we honor the life God has given us.

Whether you're stepping foot in a gym for the first time or chasing your next breakthrough, Jenny meets you exactly where you are and walks with you every step of the way — spiritually, mentally, and physically.
