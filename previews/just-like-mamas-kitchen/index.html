Our Story

Made Fresh. Seasoned Right. Every Time.

Just Like Mama's Kitchen brings the warmth of a home-cooked meal to every plate. We're known for our hand-seasoned chicken wings, thick-cut homemade fries, and juicy burgers — all made to order so every bite arrives hot and full of flavor.

Whether you stop in for a quick catfish sandwich, treat the family to our 10-piece dark chicken deal, or load up on sides like collard greens and fried okra, every plate is made with care and served with a smile.
