What We Offer

Grooming for every breed,
every dog.

From the tiniest terrier to the largest working breed, every dog that comes through the door gets the same expert attention, patience, and care. No dog too big, no coat too complex.

Full Groom

Breed-specific cut, bath, blow dry, brush out, nail trim, and ear cleaning. The complete head-to-tail package.

Bath & Brush

Deep cleaning bath with premium shampoo, conditioning treatment, professional blow dry, and thorough brush out.

Breed-Specific Cuts

Precise, breed-standard trims or custom styles tailored to how you like your dog to look. Every time, exactly right.

Nail Trim & Filing

Clean nail trim, smooth filing, and optional grinding to keep your dog comfortable and prevent overgrowth. Available as a standalone.

Ear Cleaning

Gentle, thorough ear cleaning to prevent buildup and reduce the risk of infection. Standard with every full groom.

Puppy’s First Groom

A calm, patient introduction to grooming for puppies. We take our time to make it a genuinely positive first experience.

About The Dapper Hound

Grooming you can trust.

At The Dapper Hound, every dog gets individual attention, patience, and care from an experienced groomer who genuinely loves what she does. Whether it’s a detailed breed-specific cut or a simple bath and tidy, every appointment gets the same high standard.

Dogs come in looking like dogs and leave looking their absolute best. And if your dog has been here before, they remember the warm welcome — pets and owners alike keep coming back.
