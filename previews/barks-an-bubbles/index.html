What We Offer

Every Service
Your Pet Deserves

From a quick tidy to a full transformation — we handle every breed with patience and a professional eye for detail.

Full Groom Package

Our signature service — bath, blow-dry, breed-specific haircut, nail trim, ear cleaning, and a finishing spritz. Your dog walks out looking and smelling incredible, every time.

Most Popular
Bath & Brush

Deep shampoo, conditioning rinse, blow-dry, and thorough brushing. Removes loose fur and leaves the coat gleaming with natural shine.

Nail Trim & Filing

Gentle trimming and smooth filing — including for dogs that have never let anyone near their nails. Our patient approach makes all the difference.

Ear Cleaning

Careful, thorough ear cleaning that removes buildup and reduces the risk of infection — included with every full groom or available on its own.

De-Shedding Treatment

Specialty treatment for heavy-shedding breeds. Dramatically reduces loose fur with a deshedding shampoo, deep conditioner, and high-velocity blow-out.

Our Story

Built on Patience,
Trust & Real Care

Helen and Laura opened Barks-An-Bubbles because they believed every pet deserved a groomer who genuinely cares. Located in Wichita, they've built a loyal following — including clients who make the drive from cities across Kansas — because of their calm, unhurried approach to every animal.

  • Experienced with nervous, anxious, and difficult-to-handle pets
  • By-appointment only — your pet always gets focused, unhurried attention
  • Succeeds with pets other groomers have turned away
  • All breeds welcome — terriers to large double-coated dogs
