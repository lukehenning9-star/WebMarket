Our Story

A salon built on passion,
not just grooming

Lavish Mutts was founded with one mission: to give every dog and cat the kind of attentive, professional care that makes them feel like the star they are. Located in the heart of Wichita, our salon is a calm, welcoming space where pets are treated with patience and genuine expertise.

From the very first appointment to the hundredth with a loyal member, we bring the same energy to every pet — gentle hands, sharp skills, and an eye for what makes each animal look their very best.

Whether your pup needs a quick nail trim or a full creative transformation, Lavish Mutts has you covered. Walk-in nail trims are always welcome, and online booking is open around the clock.

"Every dog deserves to feel lavish — and we make that happen, one appointment at a time."
