Our Story

Waco's Best-Kept BBQ Secret

Papa Jack's isn't trying to be fancy. It's trying to be honest — and honest means wood-fired smoke, hand-selected cuts, and sides made from scratch every single morning.

Word spread fast. What started as a quiet spot on East Waco Drive became the BBQ destination that locals guard like a treasure. When the smoke rises on Thursday morning, Waco shows up.
