About Us

Groomers who genuinely love what they do.

At Nose to Toes, every member of our team brings real skill and genuine affection for the animals in their care. Clients keep coming back — and they keep bringing their friends — because their pets leave happy and it shows.

All breeds, all sizes From compact Shih Tzus to 65-pound Labs and everything in between — we're experienced and comfortable with every dog that comes through our door.
We listen to what you want Every cut starts with a conversation. We pay attention to your preferences and communicate throughout the appointment by phone or text.
