What we offer

Full-service grooming,
for every pet.

01

Full Groom

Bath, blow-dry, breed-standard or custom haircut, ear cleaning, nail trim, and finishing. Your pet leaves looking exceptional — and smelling even better.

02

Bath & Brush

A thorough bath with professional shampoo and conditioner, full blow-dry, and brush-out. Great for maintenance between full grooms or short-coated breeds that need a proper refresh.

03

Nail Trim

Safe, precise trimming by experienced hands. We work calmly with dogs who find nail care stressful. Grinding available on request for a smooth finish.

04

Ear Cleaning

Thorough ear plucking and cleaning to remove buildup and reduce the risk of irritation or infection — especially important for floppy-eared breeds.

05

Cat Grooming

We groom cats too — and we do it well. Bathing, drying, trimming, and de-shedding handled with patience for cats of all breeds and temperaments.

Pricing varies by breed, coat condition, and size. Call (316) 267-8812 for a straightforward quote.

About us

Personal care.
Every single time.

Bonjour's Puppy Parade is a trusted name in Wichita grooming. Founded and run by Jeanette Bonjour, the studio was built on a simple belief: every pet deserves patient, skilled, genuinely attentive care — not a rushed assembly line.

We groom all breeds, dogs and cats alike. When you walk through the door, you can see every pet being groomed on an open floor. No back rooms, no locked kennels — just a calm, comfortable environment where your pet is in good hands.
