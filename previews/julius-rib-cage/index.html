The story

One place.
One rub.
Decades in.

Julius Rib Cage has been anchoring the southwest side of Wichita for decades. We run every order ourselves — slow-smoking the meats, seasoning the sides with our signature dry rub, and keeping regulars coming back year after year.

The dining room closed in 2020 and never reopened. The barbecue didn't change. Call ahead for large family orders and we'll have everything ready when you pull up.
