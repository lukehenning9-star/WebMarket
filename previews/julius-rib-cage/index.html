The story

One man.
One rub.
Decades in.

Julius Rib Cage has been anchoring the southwest side of Wichita for decades. The same owner runs every order himself — slow-smoking the meats, seasoning the sides with his signature dry rub, and keeping regulars coming back year after year.

The dining room closed in 2020 and never reopened. The barbecue didn't change. Call ahead for large family orders and he'll have everything ready when you pull up.
